26.8.17

13 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.16

Some microphones were being recorded and then transcribed as silence: fixed, and the meetings it already cost you can be transcribed again.

Fixed

  • If your microphone wasn't a plain single channel — a stereo interface, or a video-call app's virtual mic — your side of the conversation could go missing from the transcript while recording perfectly to disk. Stereo inputs were being read as their left channel only; a three-channel device was read as nothing at all. Every channel is now mixed down to what the transcriber wants, whatever the device reports. If you record through anything other than your Mac's built-in mic, this is the release that matters.

Added

  • Transcribe a meeting again from its retained audio, one channel at a time, from the meeting's page. This is how a transcript the bug above already spoiled gets recovered, so long as retention hasn't purged the audio yet. Corrections you typed by hand survive the second pass.
  • Cheerio now says when a meeting needs that: a channel that recorded real audio and produced no transcript is flagged, instead of leaving you to notice a one-sided transcript yourself.
  • A meeting being worked on says so. Re-identifying speakers, re-transcribing, or processing after a crash now shows on the meeting, in the list, and in the menu bar, with the stage it's on.

Known issues

  • Re-transcribing repairs the transcript but doesn't rewrite the summary or action items above it, so those still reflect the first pass.
  • Repair only works while the audio is there: retention decides how long a spoiled transcript can be fixed, and the default is three days.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • A live Zoom or Meet call still hasn't been checked end to end, though in-person recording has.

26.8.16

12 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.15

A call played out loud no longer transcribes itself twice, and the recording mode that was supposed to fix that is gone.

Added

  • Processing now finds the microphone's copy of the far end and keeps it out of the transcript, the summary, the speakers panel, search, and everything handed to an agent. Headphones stopped being a setup step. The retained audio and the live transcript still carry the duplicate; the processed meeting doesn't.

Removed

  • Recording modes and echo cancellation. Echo cancellation was the earlier attempt at the problem above, and on one real video call it recorded a completely silent microphone channel — losing half a meeting is worse than transcribing it twice. There's one recording behavior now and nothing to pick.

Fixed

  • Renaming or confirming a speaker no longer brings hidden duplicate lines back into the transcript.

Known issues

  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • Retained audio can't be re-transcribed from inside the app.
  • A live Zoom or Meet call still hasn't been checked end to end, though in-person recording has.

26.8.15

11 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.14

A meeting now waits for your say-so before the machines act on it, and you can steer which agent gets it.

Added

  • An optional review window when a meeting recording ends (directives skip it, and you can turn it off): add notes you didn't get to type live, set the meeting kind, and decide whether the callback fires, with an extra prompt if it does. Confirm to process now, or let the grace period (2 minutes of no edits, configurable) run it for you. Zero-touch still works: set the window to off and nothing changes.
  • Multiple named callback commands with one default. Pick a different one in the review window, or run any of them against a finished meeting from its page. Your existing command carries over as the default; nothing to reconfigure.
  • Hover a transcript line to play the audio from that moment. Works with keyboard playback controls and VoiceOver too.
  • Agents on your Mac can find the bundled MCP server on their own: the app maintains a small manifest at ~/Library/Application Support/Cheerio/mcp-server.json with the server's path and transport.
  • Every screen is now contrast-audited in CI, light and dark, before a UI change can merge.

Changed

  • Audio retention now defaults to 3 days instead of 7. If you never touched the setting, audio older than 3 days is purged on next launch; your choice is kept if you ever set one.
  • The selected meeting row is readable again: a lighter selection fill with text that clears WCAG AA, window focused or not.
  • Roughly sixty small text-contrast fixes across the app, both appearances.
  • If a recording ends with a silent microphone channel, the log now says so and names the likely causes, instead of failing quietly.

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud in In Person mode, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones. Video Call mode's echo cancellation still hasn't been verified against real speaker playback.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • Retained audio can't be re-transcribed from inside the app.

26.8.14

9 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.13

Round two of feedback recorded in Cheerio: the blank spots got useful and the speakers panel tells you who actually talked.

Added

  • The empty pane you see before selecting a meeting now shows your upcoming meetings, recent activity, a record button, and a rotating tip instead of nothing.
  • If nobody's enrolled, the app says so at launch and offers to fix it. Enrollment is what makes speaker names work, and there was no nudge toward it.
  • A mic check before you record: a live level meter so you can see the mic is hearing you, and a confirmation when enrollment actually captured your voice.
  • Each speaker now shows how long they talked and their share of the meeting, with a color-coded timeline of who spoke when. Line counts are gone.
  • Transcripts show a timestamp each minute, and a live transcript follows the newest line unless you've scrolled up (with a jump back down when you have).

Changed

  • While recording, the sidebar shows a proper stop control and calendar context that reads as information, not buttons.
  • Rename, confirm, and enroll moved behind a per-speaker menu so the panel leads with the numbers.

Fixed

  • Updating across the 26.8.12 app-identity change could greet you with an empty library while your meetings sat untouched in the old folder. The migration now moves them into place. If you've recorded new meetings since, the app leaves both libraries alone rather than guessing (recovery for that case is manual for now).

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud in In Person mode, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones. Video Call mode's echo cancellation still hasn't been verified against real speaker playback.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • Retained audio can't be re-transcribed from inside the app.

The UI overhaul: recorded as feedback in Cheerio, handed to the agents by Cheerio, shipped the same night.

Added

  • Rough notes render as Markdown once the meeting ends (headings, lists, bold, links), with an Edit toggle back to plain text. Typing during a meeting is untouched.

  • A meeting opens with one title (with a visible rename button), clear lines between sections, the transcript collapsed until you want it, and notes you can keep editing after the meeting ends.
  • The selected meeting reads as the main region; the sidebar recedes.
  • Speaker identification on a one-hour recording now uses a flat ~140 MB instead of over 2 GB.

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud in In Person mode, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones. Video Call mode's echo cancellation still hasn't been verified against real speaker playback.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.

26.8.12

9 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.11

Meetings are visible to your agents from the moment recording starts, and speaker names you confirm stay confirmed.

Added

  • A recording shows up in the library and to MCP readers the moment it starts, marked in progress, with finalized lines landing within a couple of seconds. Agents no longer wait for a meeting to end to start reading it.
  • Confirm a speaker in one click. When the model matched a name and it's right, settle the whole speaker from the speakers panel instead of renaming line by line. The dotted "not checked" ring goes away because it was checked.
  • Delete a meeting, from the list or the meeting itself, with its transcript, notes, and retained audio.
  • If the app crashes mid-recording, the transcript survives up to the last saved line, and the meeting closes out cleanly on next launch.

Changed

  • The app's identifier is now co.obvios.cheerio.mac. Your meetings, settings, and enrollment move over automatically on first launch. One consequence you'll see: macOS re-asks for the microphone, system audio, calendar, and notification permissions once, because macOS ties permissions to the identifier.
  • Your meetings folder is now ~/Library/Application Support/co.obvios.cheerio.mac.

Fixed

  • A transcript line longer than the summarizer's budget could produce a broken write-up; long lines now split cleanly and every piece keeps its speaker.

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud in In Person mode, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones. Video Call mode's echo cancellation still hasn't been verified against real speaker playback.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • A very first launch can still flash an empty window frame for a moment.
  • Retained audio can't be played back from inside the app.

26.8.11

8 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 26.8.9

The app comes to you when a meeting starts.

Added

  • A notification offers to record when a calendar meeting with other people starts (never twice for the same meeting, never while you're already recording), and another tells you when a finished meeting's notes are ready. Both switches are in the Notifications section of Settings → General.
  • The meeting list is grouped by day: Today, Yesterday, then the weekday or the date.
  • Recording modes: In Person (today's behavior, the default) and Video Call, which turns on echo cancellation for the mic. Pick one in the sidebar or the menu bar.
  • Launched from the DMG or your Downloads folder, Cheerio offers to move itself to Applications (where it can update itself). If it's already installed, it says so and points you at the installed copy instead of letting a second one pile up.
  • A visual refresh on one design system: each speaker keeps one color that survives renames and relaunches, recording indicators share one copper ring everywhere, and status messages use the same accessible palette.

Fixed

  • Version 26.8.10 shipped these same changes shortly before and crashed at launch on any Mac that had used an earlier version (a store-migration bug). It was pulled within the hour; 26.8.11 is that release with the migration fixed, and a store from any earlier version now opens cleanly.
  • On a very first launch the library window could appear alongside the walkthrough. The walkthrough is now the only window with content on first run.
  • A record-this-meeting suggestion could linger or re-offer after you'd started recording or turned the feature off. Withdrawn offers now clean up after themselves.

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud in In Person mode, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones. Video Call mode's echo cancellation exists but hasn't been verified against real speaker playback yet, so headphones are still the advice.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends.
  • A very first launch can still flash an empty window frame for a moment before the walkthrough appears.
  • Retained audio can't be played back or re-transcribed from inside the app.

26.8.9

8 August 2026 · Download · Changes since 2026.8.8

Transcripts your local AI agents can act on, from an app that now updates itself.

Added

  • Action items now say who committed to them: your own commitments are action items, everyone else's are follow-ups with a name to chase, and tools reading a meeting get the same distinction.
  • A transcript-ready callback: point Settings → Callback at any command and it runs when a meeting finishes processing, with the full transcript as JSON.
  • Give Direction… in the menu bar: record instructions for your AI assistant without pretending it's a meeting. Directives are tagged and filterable in the library.
  • A bundled MCP server, so agents on this Mac can look up your meetings themselves. Setup snippets live in Settings → Agents.
  • A first-run walkthrough that explains each permission before asking and gets your voice enrolled, so speakers come back named instead of "Speaker 2".
  • Meetings can be renamed anywhere their title appears, and recordings left on the placeholder title name themselves from the transcript.
  • The app updates itself: it checks its own releases once a day and can install updates automatically. Both switches are in Settings → Updates.
  • The download is now a DMG that does the drag-to-Applications step for you.

Fixed

  • The app asked for calendar access on every launch, even after you'd said no. It now only asks when you choose to connect the calendar.

Known issues

  • The mic hears your speakers: on a call played out loud, the transcript says everything twice. Use headphones.
  • The live transcript shows Me/Them during the meeting; names appear once it ends and speaker identification runs.
  • On a very first launch the library window can appear alongside the walkthrough instead of after it.

2026.8.8

8 August 2026 · Download · GitHub

The first build that records a real meeting end to end and gives you notes worth reading afterwards.

Added

  • Records your microphone and system audio as two separate streams, so any meeting app works and nothing joins your call.
  • Transcribes both streams on-device, live, labelled Me and Them.
  • Identifies speakers by name after the recording stops. Enroll a voice once in Settings → Participants and it comes back named instead of Speaker 2.
  • Corrects a speaker on one line or across a whole meeting. Your correction outranks the model and survives re-running identification.
  • A rough-notes scratchpad beside the live transcript, which becomes the first input to the summary rather than an afterthought.
  • Generates enhanced notes locally after the meeting: summary, key points, decisions, action items.
  • Titles recordings from your calendar, if you let it. Refusing calendar access costs you only the convenience.
  • A searchable library across titles, notes, transcripts and speaker names, and Markdown export for any meeting.
  • Audio retention you set: discard immediately, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or keep forever. Seven days is the default.
  • A menu-bar item that starts and stops a recording without opening the window.

Known issues

  • Your microphone hears your speakers. With meeting audio playing out loud it lands in both channels and the transcript duplicates itself, which skews the summary. Use headphones until echo cancellation is in.
  • The live transcript shows Me and Them, not names; identification runs over the recording once it stops. In an in-person meeting everyone is on the microphone, so every live line reads Me. It looks like a failure and isn't.
  • At most four speakers per channel. That's a hard limit of the speaker model; beyond four, voices get merged into existing slots and the app tells you who it left out rather than dropping them quietly.
  • Nothing records which side of a call someone was on, so each enrolled participant is checked against both channels and can use up a slot on the wrong one.
  • Calendar is read-only. Recordings take their title from the current event, but suggesting a recording when a meeting starts, and attaching notes back to the event, aren't built.
  • Retained audio can't be played back yet. It's written and purged on schedule, but there's no way to listen to it.
  • Never tested against a live video call. In-person recording is verified; a Zoom or Meet call is not.
